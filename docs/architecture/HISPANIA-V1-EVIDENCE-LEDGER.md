## Status vocabulary

- **VERIFIED:** directly checked at the identified source SHA/environment.
- **VERIFIED WITH LIMITATIONS:** checked, with a narrower scope than the product/architecture objective.
- **HISTORICAL EVIDENCE:** reported or checked against an earlier source SHA or separate worktree; not current-HEAD validation.
- **NOT RUN:** no execution evidence was found for the stated scope.
- **BLOCKED:** a named prerequisite prevented a valid run.
- **FAILED:** a valid test or check ran and failed its intended assertion.
- **PLANNED:** approved direction or future work without an implementation/acceptance result.
